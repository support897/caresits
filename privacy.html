Privacy Policy

Caresits — last updated 14 October 2026

The short version

This version of the app keeps everything on your device. It has no account, no sign in, and no server of its own. It does not collect, send, sell, or share your information. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking in the app, and the app itself makes no network calls at all.

What the app keeps, and where

Anything you type or add — your profile answers, child profiles, Day Story entries, roster, timesheets, documents you note, messages in demo threads, settings, and a background photo if you choose one — is saved only in the app's storage on your device. If you clear the app's data or delete the app, that information goes with it, because it never existed anywhere else.

Demo profiles

Some editions of the app come with example carer profiles, reviews, and message threads so you can see how matching and the Day Story work. They are marked Demo where they appear. They are not real people, and no real person's information is in them beyond published style examples created for the demo. The store edition comes with none of these: it starts empty.

Children's information

Children do not use this app. Adults write about children in it: names, ages, routines, food, sleep, health notes and photos the adult chooses to note. In this version all of that stays on the adult's device, under the adult's control. Child profiles are never shown in search or matching inside this version. Photos you pick from your device are shown inside the app only and are not uploaded anywhere.

Links out of the app

The Trust Centre links to official check registers and official rule pages for your country. Those links open in your browser, only when you tap them. The sites you visit there have their own privacy policies, and anything you type on those sites goes to them, not to us.

Your choices

  • Download my data: Settings has a button that saves a copy of everything the app holds for you, as a file.
  • Start again: Settings can clear this device. The app asks first and names what will be cleared, because clearing is final.
  • Notifications: reminders are local to your device. Nothing is sent to you from a server.

Changes to this policy

If a later version adds accounts or online features, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the app's store listings will be re-checked against it. The date at the top of this page always shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the developer contact shown on the app store listing page for this app.
