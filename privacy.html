Privacy Policy

Caresitters — last updated 17 October 2026

The short version

Caresitters helps families and carers find each other. To do that, the app holds an account for you and keeps what you put in it. We never sell your information. There are no ads in the app, no advertising trackers, and no analytics watching how you use it. You can delete your account inside the app at any time, and deletion happens straight away.

Who is responsible

The developer of Caresitters is the data controller for the information the app holds. Questions about this policy go to the developer contact shown on the app store listing page for this app.

Your account

You join with Google, Apple, or your email address and a password you choose. We hold your email address, the name you give the app, and your account ID. Sign in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. If you look around in the demo first, that stays on your device and no account is made.

What you put in the app

Your profile and your answers to the matching questions are held under your account. So are the checks you choose to share, your child profiles and Day Story entries, your pair calendars, rosters and timesheets, and the messages you send. Settings like your background choice are kept on your device, and a background photo you upload stays on your device too. A draft of your questionnaire answers is kept on your device while you work on it.

Where it lives

Account information is stored with Google Firebase, in Australia (the Sydney region). Some things the app holds on the device itself, as the section above says.

What other users can see

Members who are signed in can browse profiles. A profile shows your name, your postcode area, your photo if you add one, and the matching points from your answers. The map shows the postcode area only, never an exact address. The app never asks for your device location. Child profiles are never shown in search or matching: they live in your private space only, as the next section explains. Messages open only after both sides have completed their profiles, and a conversation is visible only to the two people in it.

Children's information

Children are never users of this app. Adults enter child profiles: a first name, a theme, and the notes a carer needs, like routines, food and sleep. Day Story entries are notes about the day, written by the adult caring for the child. All of this stays in the family's private account space. It is never shown in search or matching, never sold, and never used for advertising.

Reports and blocks

Every profile card carries Report and Block. A report sends a copy of what you reported, with the category you picked and any words you added, to the operator as a safety case. The person reported is not told. Blocking someone removes each of you from the other's view at once and ends any match and shared calendar between you. The other person is never told they were blocked. Your block list is private to your account.

How long we keep things

Your information is kept while your account exists. The app never deletes your information on its own. If you delete your account, your profile, answers, child profiles, Day Story, calendars and account details are removed straight away. A safety report you filed stays with the operator as a case record, so the operator can act on it.

Your choices

  • Delete my account: the account page deletes your account and the information listed above, straight away. It cannot be undone.
  • A copy of your information: the app does not yet offer a one tap download. If you would like a copy of what we hold about you, write to the developer contact on the store listing page.
  • Your profile: you can change your answers and your profile inside the app at any time.

Links out of the app

The app links to official check registers and official rule pages for your country. Those links open in your browser, only when you tap them. The sites you visit there have their own privacy policies, and anything you type on those sites goes to them, not to us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page before it applies, and the date at the top will show the latest version.

Contact
