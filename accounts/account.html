Delete your account

Only you can delete your account. Caresitters never deletes your data for you. Deleting removes your profile, your children, your Day Story, your documents and your messages from your account, for good. If you share a hire with a carer, their own copy of that hire stays theirs.

Before you delete, you can download your data from inside the app. If you are signed in, the button below does the deletion now. If you cannot sign in, write to us from the email on your account and we will verify it is you and delete the account within 30 days.
