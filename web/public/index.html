How it works

Three easy steps. No fuss, no walls of forms.

1

Create your account

Join in under a minute with your email or Google. Your account is saved forever and works in the Caresitters app too.

2

Tell us what you need

Families share ages, days and the kind of care they want. Carers share experience, skills and when they are free.

3

Meet your match

Message, plan the roster together and book. Families can change bookings, and everyone can see the plan in the family hub.

Why parents pick Caresitters

Other apps charge to message, take a cut, or ask lots of questions first. We do none of that.

Your match level, in plain sight

Explore, Quick Connect, Better Match and Full Match. A Perfect Pair shows only when the match is real.

Urgent care board

Safe by design

Trust is earned in the open, not promised in small print.

Real profiles

Every carer profile shows experience, skills and checks level, so families can see what a carer brings before the first message.

Messages stay in the app

Bookings are tracked

Every booking is counted. Completion is tracked, cancellations are marked, and reviews stay on the profile for the next family to read.

You stay in control

Families can change bookings, and everyone can propose roster changes. Only agreed changes go ahead.
