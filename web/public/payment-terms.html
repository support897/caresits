Payment and cancellation terms

Version 2026-11-25. These terms apply to every booking paid through Caresitters. By paying, or by setting up payouts, you agree to them.

1. How payment works

  • The family pays when the carer accepts. Caresitters holds the money using Stripe.
  • After the shift, the carer taps Request payment. The family taps Release, or Report a problem.
  • If the family does nothing, the money is released to the carer 24 hours after the request. A reminder is sent at 12 hours.
  • If the carer never requests payment, the family is refunded 48 hours after the shift ends.
  • Pay only inside Caresitters. Cash and outside payments are not protected, and may end your account.

2. Fees

  • The family pays the care price plus a service and payment fee of 15% of the care price plus A$1.00. This covers Caresitters and the card and payout costs charged by Stripe.
  • The carer keeps 100% of their rate, less any amount owed under section 5.
  • The fee is shown before you pay.

4. Problems and disputes

  • If a family reports a problem, the money is frozen. Both sides can add notes.
  • Caresitters decides within 2 working days: full refund, part refund or release to the carer. Its decision is final inside the app.
  • Report a problem within 24 hours of the carer's payment request. After money is released, refunds are only given for fraud or by law.

5. Carer responsibilities

  • Carers must finish Stripe identity checks before they can be paid. The first payout can take longer while Stripe checks the account.
  • Payouts to carers are delayed by 7 days from release.
  • Each carer cancellation or no-show is a strike. Strikes lower your completion rate, which families can see. 3 strikes pause new bookings for 14 days. 5 strikes close the account (you can appeal by email). Money already earned for finished jobs is still paid out.
  • If a family's bank reverses a payment because of the carer's conduct, or the carer was already paid for a booking that is later refunded, the carer agrees to repay that amount. Caresitters may take it from future payouts. Chargeback fees charged by Stripe, currently A$25 each, may be passed on to the party at fault.

6. Family responsibilities

  • Families must not start a bank dispute for a booking without first using Report a problem.
  • If a family starts a bank dispute without a reason, Caresitters may pass on the dispute fee and close the account.
  • Each booking is limited to A$500 at first. Ask us if you need more.

7. Au pairs

Au pairs are not paid through Caresitters. Pocket money is agreed and paid directly between the au pair and the host family. Au pairs pay nothing. Host families may pay a one-time match fee when they confirm a match, shown before paying. The fee is refunded in full if the au pair cancels before arriving.

8. Limits

Caresitters is a platform that connects families and carers. Carers are not Caresitters employees. Payments are processed by Stripe and are subject to Stripe's terms.
