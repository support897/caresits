# HER RULINGS — CareSpace (one page, travels inside the build pack)

Every standing ruling Ilse has given for CareSpace, in her words where
recorded. The builder meets these INSIDE the work. A build round does not
start until the desk files a READ PROOF naming this page and the
brand-of-record files it read, with their fingerprints.

## Name
- The app is named **CareSpace** and nothing else, everywhere
  (Ilse, 2026-11-11: "leave it only as carespace"). "Daily Care" never.

## Door (2026-11-11)
- First page shows ONLY Google and Apple, plus ONE small email button.
- Email is its own page: email + the user's own chosen password, sign up
  and sign in there, no unnecessary text. An email LINK is not a door.
- Email sign-in NEVER silently creates an account.
- Sign-up asks the user's name and saves it to their profile. Greetings
  use the signed-in user's OWN saved name only.
- A cancel is not an error: cancelling Google or Apple sign-in, or
  walking back from either, returns to a clean door, no red line.
- No reference to Ilse anywhere in the app, except her own account
  showing her own data.
- GOOGLE FIRST (2026-11-12 23:33): whenever a sign up or login step
  comes up, check for a Google option first and use it (the team's own
  test Google account for team testing).

## Colours (Way B, 2026-11-12; corrected 2026-11-13)

  of blue and yellow shades. NO dark green ever. No green/mint wash
