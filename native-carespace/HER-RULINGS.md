## Bunny and art rules
- Her bunny is drawn art, exactly as drawn, never redrawn, and is NOT the logo.
- NOTHING is ever overlaid on the bunny: no icon, badge, loop or text on her, any screen.
- When the bunny is with an object she is drawn HOLDING it in her paws as part of the artwork.
- Logo and icon work is real graphic artwork only. No code built shapes, no AI raster drafts as logos (the old logo-bubbles webps are WITHDRAWN, never show or use).
