01. Non-negotiable design principles

  1. Branding: a complete brand manual governs purpose, marks, exclusions, palette, typography, spacing, iconography, motion, imagery, voice, accessibility and ulilac examples. Source: Adobe.
  2. Psychology: autonomy, competence and relatedness support high-quality motivation. Use user-controlled plans, truthful progress and meaningful family connection. Source: Deci and Ryan.
  3. Design: Apple Design Award examples show focused interactions, inclusive UI, intentional feedback and coherent visuals; use native tab bars for navigation and toolbars for actions. Sources: Apple Design Awards, 2026 awards, Apple HIG.

06. Shared account, permissions and persistence

  • One account identity across both native apps. Use Sign in with Apple and other configured providers with a common backend uid. iOS Keychain stores secrets; do not share raw credentials between app sandboxes.
  • Canonical child records belong to the parent. CareSpace links children into specific care spaces only with explicit parent consent. Do not duplicate child records on each login.
  • Role is explicit and per-app navigation follows the active role. Users with both roles can switch safely.
  • CareSpace spaces are isolated by membership; carers serving multiple families must never read cross-family children, photos, chats, schedules or medical data.
  • Allergy, medication and emergency details are parent-controlled. Carers can log observations but not overwrite medical facts.
  • Bookings and change requests are server-authoritative. Both parties approve changes to agreed terms.
  • Photos strip EXIF, use access-controlled storage and preserve audience consent. Deleting or revoking a member removes access.
  • Sync supports offline drafts, retry and explicit pending indicators. No silent data loss.
