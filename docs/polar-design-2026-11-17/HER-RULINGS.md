## Name and voice
Name: Caresitters. Tagline: "Find care. Share the day." Door line: "Join Caresitters."
Voice: kind, cute, Apple simple, concrete facts. Australian spelling. No dashes in any copy. No invented families, carers, reviews or safety claims, ever. The word Caresits appears on zero public surfaces.

## Door law (new shape, 11 Nov 2026)
The first page shows ONLY Google and Apple, plus ONE small email button. Email is its own page: email plus the user's own chosen password, sign up and sign in there. An email link is not a door. Google and Apple consent sheets title "Caresitters", never a project number. The door never shows a raw system error; a cancel or a dismissed flow returns quietly.

## Swipe law (11 Nov 2026)
Sliding back returns to the previous page on every screen EXCEPT the swipe deck, where sliding is the matching itself.

## Themes (10 Nov 2026)
Every app offers light, dark, beige, and a brand colour theme. White is the default. The pick rides the account and must survive a fresh install restore. For Caresitters the brand colour theme is the pastel brand above.

## Product laws that ride every build
Sign up asks the user's own name, always, no skip on the name step. No reference to Ilse anywhere in the user app. Chat opens on the match; the Book button stays; chat never waits for a booking. Messages open fast, proven by timed opens on the exact build. Icons are luxurious and designed for the brand; no emojis anywhere in the app. Sidebar: grouped areas, one open at a time; menu open means the content shrinks to a sliver. Delete is proven by an owner side read of the full footprint, never by access death alone.

Her words: "All settings SHOULD BE IN SETTINGS not in me like bunny and look, same for all apps so fix it". Every setting lives in a Settings area, never in Me/Profile: the bunny setting, the look/theme pick, and every other setting-shaped control. A setting found in Me = Not pass. Me keeps the person: name, profile, account facts.
