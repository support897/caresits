## Voice (locked sheet, Content Boss custody, 17 Oct 2026)
Kind, cute, and Apple simple, with every claim made of concrete facts. Warm · Plain · Concrete · Calm · Fair.
Words we use: checked · shown · the register · the date · the day · shared · fair · free for carers · local.
Words we never use: safe as a bare claim · trusted as self praise · vetted · screened · top rated · best · hype words of any kind.
Standing lines: "Find care. Share the day." · "Every badge shows what was checked, and when." · No dashes in any copy. Australian spelling.

## The feel
A steady hand. Warm, careful, never salesy. Calm is a feature: the app is built to lower a parent's heart rate, and the brand sounds like the app feels. Trust is the brand: every badge shows what was checked and when; no invented families, carers, reviews or safety claims, ever; no child is content.
Values: honesty you can see · fair on both sides of the door (carers free forever) · children stay private · local first · calm as a feature.
