## Mascot — the Bunny
- Pink `#F4A9BC`, heart on the belly, soft ears.
- One layered vector component, transparent, under 60 KB.
- 7 moods: hello · point · celebrate · encourage · oops · rest · think.
- Always gently moving (breathing, blinking, ear twitch, eye follow,
  hop on tap) unless reduced motion is on or the user turns the bunny down.
- Never blocks a tap, never nags.

## Voice
- A calm daily tool, like a messenger built for childcare.
- Plain words, short sentences, kind. Australian plain English.
- No fake scarcity, no manipulative notifications, ever.
- "Verified" is shown only after a real check happened.
- AI writes words and suggests; it never decides safety, allergies,
  medication or who is qualified.

## Feel (FEEL.md)
Premium, playful, modern. Soft pink + warm cream + fresh green accent.
Editorial type, rounded cards, soft shadows, spring motion, floating
bottom nav, generous spacing. Dopamine from a satisfying experience,
never from manipulation.

## Themes (Ilse, 11 Nov 2026, all-apps law — applies to CareSpace)

- The user can change it any time. The pick must survive a restore.
